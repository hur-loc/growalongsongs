Grow-Along Songs

Original sing-along songs for little ones aged 2 to 5. Every episode is one song, one story and four words to learn. Season 1 follows Pip the little seed and Lina the ladybug through a year in the garden.

Watch on YouTube

New songs every Tuesday and Saturday.

How the songs are made

Every episode is written, checked and edited by a person. Images, animation and music are created with AI tools. All videos are marked as made for kids and labelled as containing synthetic media.

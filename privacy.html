Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Who we are

Grow-Along Songs is an independent children's music channel based in Switzerland. Contact: growalongsongs@gmail.com.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and collects no personal data. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our YouTube channel

Our videos are published on YouTube and are marked as made for kids. When you watch them, YouTube processes data under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal data about viewers; we only see aggregated statistics provided by YouTube.

The "YT Kids Uploader" application

"YT Kids Uploader" is an internal tool that we use to upload and manage videos on our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.

  • It accesses only the Google account and YouTube channel of Grow-Along Songs, after explicit consent by the channel owner.
  • It uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and read the channel's own statistics.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the channel owner's computer and are never shared with third parties.
  • It does not collect, store or process any data about viewers or other YouTube users.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The application uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you may request information about, correction of or deletion of personal data concerning you. Write to growalongsongs@gmail.com.

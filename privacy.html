Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Who we are

Grow-Along Songs is a children's music channel based in Switzerland. Contact: growalongsongs@gmail.com.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and collects no personal data. It is hosted by GitHub; technical data such as IP addresses may be processed by the hosting provider under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our YouTube channel

Our videos are published on YouTube. When you watch them, YouTube processes data under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal data about viewers.

Use of Google APIs

Grow-Along Songs uses YouTube API Services to publish and manage content on its own YouTube channel. Access is granted through Google's OAuth consent and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data obtained through these APIs is used solely to manage the Grow-Along Songs channel. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used for any other purpose. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Use of YouTube API Services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Email

If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply to you and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where applicable, the GDPR, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal data concerning you. Write to growalongsongs@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
